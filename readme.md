# Jeep Adventure Picker for New Smyrna Beach and Daytona Beach

A free Jeep day planner by Michelle. Choose a coastal, local history, or responsible trail theme and a time window to get an outing idea and packing list.

## What it does

A Jeep day can be a short coastal drive, a walk through local history, or a carefully planned route on legal public roads. This tool helps you choose a theme and remember what to bring.

## How to use it

Open the published website, choose your mood and available time, and get a suggested plan and packing checklist. Always check current weather, road access, and local rules before leaving.

## About Michelle

Michelle shares Jeep adventure ideas for New Smyrna Beach, Daytona Beach, and the surrounding Florida coast. This independent project is for people who enjoy exploring the area and planning a fun day out.

This project is not affiliated with Jeep or its manufacturer.
